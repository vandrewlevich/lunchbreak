Touchpad Practice

Practice moving, pointing, clicking, and scrolling.

Take your time. Accuracy matters more than speed.

1. Scroll Down

Use two fingers on the touchpad. Slide them down.

Your goal: Go down the page and find the yellow duck.

Keep scrolling. The duck is farther down.
You are doing it. Keep going.
🦆

You found the duck!

Now try scrolling back up a little, then down again.

3. Click

Click means to press down on the touchpad. Use one finger.

Your message will appear here.

Remember: Move → Stop → Click

4. Right-Click

Put two fingers on the touchpad and tap once.

Your goal: Right-click the camera.

Right-click the camera.

Tip: A two-finger tap on a Chromebook is a right-click.

5. Scroll and Click

Scroll down until you find the red balloon. Then click it once.

The balloon is not here yet.
Keep scrolling down.
Click the balloon.

6. Highlight Text

Highlighting means selecting words with the touchpad.

Your goal: Highlight the words practice makes progress in the sentence below.

How:

1. Move the pointer to the beginning of the word practice.

2. Press down on the touchpad with one finger and keep holding.

3. While holding, move your other finger across the words practice makes progress.

4. Let go when the words are highlighted.

Learning new computer skills takes time, and practice makes progress every day.

If you highlight too much or too little, click once in a blank area and try again.

7. Final Practice

1. Point to the star without clicking it.

Keep scrolling.

Click the check mark when you are ready.
