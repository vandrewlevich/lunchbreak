Keyboard Practice

Follow along with your instructor. Type in each box.

1. Type Your Name

Click in the box. Type your first name.

2. Use the Space Bar

Type your first and last name. Press the space bar between the names.

3. Make a Capital Letter

Hold down the Shift key and press a letter.

My name is Maria.

4. Use Backspace

Type the word below. Make one mistake on purpose. Then use Backspace to fix it.

computer

5. Press Enter

Type your first name. Press Enter. Then type your last name.

6. Type a Sentence

Type the sentence below.

I am learning how to use a Chromebook.

7. Highlight Text

Type the sentence below. Then highlight the word Chromebook.

I am using a Chromebook.

Use the touchpad to drag across the word.

8. Copy and Paste

Type your name in the first box. Highlight it and copy it. Then paste it into the second box.

Copy: Ctrl + C     Paste: Ctrl + V
