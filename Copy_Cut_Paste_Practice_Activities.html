Copy, Cut, and Paste Practice

You will learn 2 different ways to copy, cut and paste. One will be with the keyboard and one with the right-click menu. REMEMBER: To use right click, you use 2 fingers and touch the middle of the touchpad.

Keyboard: Copy and Paste

1. Copy One Word

Highlight library.

Press Ctrl + C.

Click in the box.

Press Ctrl + V.

library
Copy library and paste it into the box.
Keyboard: Copy and Paste

2. Copy a Phrase

Copy the words computer class and paste them into the box.

I enjoy my computer class every week.
Use Ctrl + C and Ctrl + V.
Keyboard: Cut and Paste

3. Cut One Word

Highlight Tuesday.

Press Ctrl + X.

Click in the box.

Press Ctrl + V.

Tuesday
Cut Tuesday and paste it into the box.
Keyboard: Cut and Paste

4. Cut a Phrase

Cut the words send an email and paste them into the box.

Today I need to send an email before lunch.
Use Ctrl + X and Ctrl + V.
Right-Click Menu: Copy and Paste

5. Copy with Right-Click

Highlight appointment.

Right-click the highlighted word.

Choose Copy from the menu.

Right-click inside the box and choose Paste.

appointment
Use the right-click menu to copy and paste.
Right-Click Menu: Cut and Paste

6. Cut with Right-Click

Highlight phone number.

Choose Cut.

Right-click inside the box and choose Paste.

Please write down your phone number.

Right-Click Menu: Copy and Paste

You learned 2 ways to copy, cut, and paste.

Keyboard

Copy: Ctrl + C

Cut: Ctrl + X

Paste: Ctrl + V

Right-Click Menu

Highlight the text, then right-click it.

Choose Copy or Cut from the menu.

Right-click where you want the text to go and choose Paste.
